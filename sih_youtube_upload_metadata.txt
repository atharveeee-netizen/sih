OVERVIEW:
Beekeeping in India faces two existential challenges: 
1. Catastrophic hive losses caused by swarming, pests, and thermal distress (Colony Collapse Disorder).
2. Rampant adulteration and counterfeit packaging across the unorganized honey supply chain, diminishing farmer income and consumer trust.

OUR COMPLETE SOLUTION:

PART 1: ON-NODE EDGE AI SENSING & CYBER-PHYSICAL MONITORING (00:00 - 04:54)
- Custom STM32F401RE embedded sensing node with dual MEMS acoustic arrays (I2S) and DS18B20 1-Wire multi-point thermal arrays.
- On-node CMSIS-DSP 512-point Real FFT processing executed in 2.8ms, consuming under 28mW.
- Autonomous real-time detection of Queen Piping (300-500 Hz acoustic resonance) and swarming precursors before hive departure.
- LoRaWAN long-range sub-GHz telemetry transmitting to an edge Raspberry Pi gateway with offline SQLite buffering.
- Full mechanical Langstroth hive CAD integration validated through ANSYS acoustic resonance and thermal dissipation simulations.
- Trilingual offline-first Progressive Web App (PWA) available in English, Hindi, and Marathi, empowering smallholder apiculturists.

PART 2: HONEYCHAIN BLOCKCHAIN PROVENANCE & TRACEABILITY (04:54 - 08:53)
- Cryptographic supply chain verification deployed on the Polygon PoS network.
- Dual-EOA (Externally Owned Account) batch minting protocol: requires joint cryptographic signatures from both the Harvester and Certified Government/Testing Officers.
- Permanent decentralized metadata pinning via IPFS ensuring immutable harvest records, lab chemical purity tests, and geographic origins.
- Dynamic anti-counterfeit QR code generation tied uniquely to tamper-evident tamper-proof jar seals.
- Instant, zero-installation consumer mobile scanning interface verifying hive origin, moisture levels, harvest dates, and FSSAI compliance.
